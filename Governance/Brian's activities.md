## Invoicing for services rendered
- NDIS
	- Maintain records of services provided 
		- Receive written reports from folks like Bob who actually provide the service
		- This is recent requirement to provide for potential audit
	- Send regular invoices to clients’ registered providers
		- There are relevant invoice templates in the NDIS folder of the *MS365* repository.
		- Held in *Excel* format and sent as PDF to avoid format issues or accidental alteration. 
- For non-NDIS invoices (eg for grants) there are similar templates.
## Recording income & expenditure
- In addition to the *GnuCash* transaction records, it’s appropriate that we keep receipts etc. 
	- Debit card transactions need to be recorded in a way that can be understood
	- Members will regularly provide receipts and expect reimbursement
- My practice has been to hold the records as PDFs for easy reviewing.
	- Incoming emails get saved as PDFs
	- Paper documents get photographed and saved as PDF 
## Document storage
All the files are held on the *MS365* `Committee` repository
- Most are within the `Treasurer` top level folder except for the NDIS ones.
- Where spreadsheets have been used they are readable by either *Excel* or *LibreOffice Calc*. Saving from *LO Calc* back to *Excel* format seems to work well.
