We often receive requests via our public website or facebook that require some kind of response.

# “I want do xxx” type enquiries
The kind of enquiry that seeks information about how we do stuff is best responded to with a brief answer to the question and an invitation to call in on a Visitors Night to check us out. A good example is something like this:
```
Dear <name>,
We can certainly help you with getting started with 3D printing. We have 3 printers one of which we lend to members.

Just come along on a Visitors Night (most Thursday evenings) to meet the members. We will give you a tour and answer any questions.
```

# Facebook group membership requests
- If someone applies to join the Facebook members group, who is a paid-up member, we automatically let them in.
- When someone who’s not a paid-up member asks to join the group, they get denied. We usually `Decline with feedback` — one of the options under the three dots on the *Member requests* page. The standard feedback message is something like:
```
I'm sorry, <name>, but as is clearly stated in the first line of its About panel, this group is for paid-up members of Hobart Hackerspace only.

If you'd like to join, check out our website at https://hobarthackerspace.org.au and come along on a Visitors Night (most Thursday evenings) to meet the members.
```

# Jobbing requests
We often receive requests via our public website for us to perform jobbing requests (Performing some technical or machining task, with payment for services), We are not a jobbing shop and carry no suitable insurance as an organisation.

The standard response to such requests is of the form:
```
Sorry, <name>, but the Hackerspace is not a service provider and does not do jobbing work. It is a co-operative group to share tools and ideas. 

If you'd like to see more, check out our website https://hobarthackerspace.org.au for times that we are open to visitors.
```
